Solidra CAD Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026.

Solidra CAD doesn't collect any data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server.

The parts you create are files on your device, or in a storage location you choose, such as iCloud Drive. They're shared only if you export or send them yourself.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
