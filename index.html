Solidra CAD

Parametric 3D modeling for iPad and Mac

Draw a 2D sketch, constrain it, turn it into a solid, and change any dimension later: the whole part rebuilds. Export STL and OBJ for 3D printing and other tools.

Support

Questions, bugs or feature ideas: open an issue.

What this version doesn't do

Solidra CAD models solids as polygon meshes. Fillets, chamfers, shells and STEP or IGES exchange aren't available yet. It runs on iPad and Mac, not iPhone.

Where are my files?

Parts are ordinary .solidra files. On iPad they're in the Files app; on Mac, wherever you saved them.
